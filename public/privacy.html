Privacy Notice

Last updated: 1 November 2026

GlobalRate is a free platform for live currency rates and a community marketplace (money exchange, rooms, rentals and items). This notice explains in plain language what personal data we collect, why, how long we keep it and what your rights are.

1. Who is responsible

GlobalRate is run by Mighty Samson (MightSmith).
Contact for any privacy question or request: admin@globalrate.space

2. What we collect

DataWhy we need it
Account details: name, email, phone number, and a password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form, so nobody can read it)To create and secure your account and let you post and message
Listings you post: text, price, city or area, country, photos, and for rooms a street addressTo show your listing to other users
Messages you send to other users through the siteTo let buyers, sellers and flatmates talk to each other
Basic activity records: a daily count of site visits (no personal details) and a log of sign-ups, logins, failed logins and listing activity, which contains your name or email but no IP addressTo keep the platform secure, detect abuse and see how the site is used
Emails we send you: welcome, email confirmation, password reset and a confirmation when you post a listingTo run your account and keep you informed about your own activity

We do not store visitors' IP addresses. To block repeated sign-in attempts and spam, the server briefly counts requests per IP address in temporary memory; this is never saved, logged or linked to your account. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical server logs under its own policy.

We do not sell your data, we do not show adverts and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We do not use analytics services.

3. What other people can see

  • Anyone can see your listings, your first name or display name on them, and the country and city or area you chose.
  • Your phone number and a room's street address are shown only to people who are signed in.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Messages are visible only to you and the person you wrote to, and to the site owner when needed to deal with abuse or a complaint.

4. Legal basis

  • Providing the service (contract): account, listings, messages and service emails.
  • Legitimate interests: security, preventing fraud and abuse, and counting visits. You can object to this (see section 8).
  • Legal obligations, where the law requires us to keep or hand over data.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account details, listings and messages: until you ask us to delete your account. You can remove your own listings yourself at any time.
  • Activity log (sign-ups, logins, failed logins, listing events): the most recent 1,000 events only.
  • Password reset and email confirmation links: expire automatically after a short time.
  • Backups: the owner may keep a backup copy of the database. Deleted data leaves backups when they are replaced.

6. Who we share data with

We only use service providers needed to run the site, and only for that purpose:

  • Hosting: Render, where the site and its database run.
  • Email: Zoho Mail, which sends our emails to you.
  • Fonts: Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to load the site's fonts, so Google can see your IP address.
  • Live exchange rates: our server requests rates from a rates provider. No personal data is sent.

Some providers may process data outside the EU/EEA. Where they do, they should rely on legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. We may also disclose data if a court or authority legally requires it.

7. Browser storage

The site stores a few small items in your browser so it works: your sign-in session, your light or dark theme and your chosen country. These are needed for the site to work and are not used for tracking.

8. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • give you a copy of your data,
  • correct data that is wrong,
  • delete your account and data,
  • restrict how we use your data, or object to it,
  • give you your data in a portable format.

Email admin@globalrate.space from the address on your account. We will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority in your own country.

9. Safety and responsibility

GlobalRate is a public board that connects people. We are not a party to any exchange, rental or sale and cannot guarantee any listing or user. Meet in public places, never send money in advance to someone you have not met and use the 🚩 Report button on anything suspicious. We protect your data with measures such as hashed passwords, limited sign-in attempts, session timeouts and restricted admin access, but no online service can be 100% secure.

10. Children

GlobalRate is not meant for people under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, for big changes, tell you on the site.
